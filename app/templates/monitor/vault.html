Read this before adding an account

Use session cookies, not passwords. Log into the platform yourself in a browser, then paste the cookies here. Your password never touches this app, 2FA is already satisfied, and a stolen cookie expires on its own. Storing a password means storing full account control, and the automated login is what actually triggers lockouts.

Never use a personal account. Meta, TikTok and LinkedIn detect automated sessions well. Expect a throwaway account to get checkpointed within hours or days. That is the platform working as designed, not a bug here — the fetcher will tell you plainly when it happens.

This encrypts the database, not the running app. Secrets are encrypted at rest with a key derived from your passphrase, which is never written to disk. Once unlocked, the key is in memory. It cannot protect you from a compromised machine.
